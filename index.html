Business Turnaround Plan

Courts Plus Health Club

Addressing Challenges and Driving Sustainable Growth

Executive Summary

Courts Plus Health Club is at a critical crossroads, having recorded a $38,567 loss in 2024. These negative margins are exacerbated by high fixed costs—such as $63,032 in insurance and $62,913 in taxes—as well as an aging infrastructure requiring over $100,000 in urgent repairs. Additionally, the club’s dependency on manual processes is driving up labor expenses, while restricted operating hours limit the potential for generating new revenue. As modern facilities offering 24/7 access and digital platforms continue to enter the market, Courts Plus risks further member attrition and diminishing profitability unless decisive steps toward modernization are taken.

Current Challenges

The most pressing challenge is financial: the club's ongoing losses and its significant fixed overhead are draining profitability. Moreover, aging equipment and facilities not only require immediate funding but also deter new members seeking more contemporary amenities. Meanwhile, inefficiencies in day-to-day operations—driven largely by manual processes—continue to push labor costs higher. If left unaddressed, these issues will further erode the club’s ability to compete with newer gyms that offer advanced, tech-driven experiences.

Turnaround Strategy

To reverse these trends, Courts Plus must focus on a comprehensive modernization plan that emphasizes both cost reduction and revenue growth. Automation lies at the heart of this strategy, from self-service member check-in systems to streamlined scheduling software, which together reduce labor expenses and free staff to focus on high-value tasks. Introducing 24/7 access will expand membership opportunities and attract demographics—including local military personnel—who demand flexible hours. Enhancing marketing efforts through targeted digital campaigns will further boost membership, capitalizing on the club’s premium location and dual-pool amenities. Finally, essential facility upgrades will not only address pressing safety and maintenance needs but also position the club to justify premium pricing in line with a modern fitness environment.

Investment Required

With a purchase price of $2.6M, Courts Plus remains attractively valued against its $3.15M appraisal. To execute this turnaround, approximately $150,000 is earmarked for key facility updates, focusing on both immediate repairs and aesthetic enhancements. An additional $64,400 will fund automation systems that enable digital access control and member management, while $100,000 in working capital provides the flexibility to address unforeseen expenses. Collectively, these investments lay the foundation for a leaner cost structure, improved member experience, and renewed profitability in an increasingly competitive fitness market.

II. Current Business Analysis

Operational Roadblocks

Courts Plus Health Club is burdened by a host of operational inefficiencies that drive up expenses and hinder growth. Manual processes dominate essential tasks such as member check-ins, billing, and maintenance scheduling, resulting in significant labor overhead. This traditional approach diverts staff from high-impact customer service and strategic initiatives, creating an environment where day-to-day firefighting takes precedence over forward-thinking improvements. Compounding these labor-intensive processes are hefty monthly utility bills totaling $7,500, which, combined with outdated equipment and facilities, continue to erode the bottom line.

Additionally, a backlog of urgent repairs underscores the club’s strained infrastructure. Notable costs include $51,734 for the main building roof, $39,563 for the indoor pool roof, $4,747 for gutter replacements, and $3,495 for pool chair upgrades. Postponing these issues is a risky gamble: unchecked maintenance needs can escalate quickly, leading to larger capital outlays and potentially jeopardizing member safety. Further limiting Courts Plus’s earning potential are restrictive operating hours that fail to capture the growing demand for 24/7 access, a competitive standard in today’s modern fitness market.

Competitive Landscape & Market Opportunities

While Courts Plus boasts a prime location and extensive amenities—including dual pools, spacious courts, and a variety of fitness programs—these advantages remain underutilized. Newer, tech-savvy competitors are adept at offering digitally integrated experiences that seamlessly merge convenience, automation, and on-demand scheduling. This contrast makes it more difficult for Courts Plus to retain younger, mobile-first consumers who expect a gym to align with their dynamic lifestyles.

Despite the fierce competition, the market still offers tremendous upside. The existing membership base is sizable and loyal, but it skews older, signaling a need for fresh engagement strategies to attract younger demographics and families. Perhaps most importantly, only 20.3% of members hail from the local military community—a significant missed opportunity given the club’s close proximity to military facilities. By updating operational processes, investing in targeted marketing, and capitalizing on Courts Plus’s unique amenities, there is untapped potential to expand market share and revitalize the club’s financial performance.

III. Modernization Opportunities

Boosting Efficiency Through Automation

Courts Plus Health Club has the chance to dramatically improve efficiency and cut operating expenses by embracing modern technology. Automated access control systems, which enable 24/7 facility entry, can significantly reduce staffing requirements while catering to members who prefer non-traditional workout hours. Likewise, digital membership management software streamlines both enrollment and payment processing, eliminating the time-consuming manual tasks currently handled by on-site staff. Further staffing optimization can be achieved through automated scheduling and self-service kiosks, allowing front-desk personnel to focus on high-value interactions and member retention.

Substantial savings can also be realized by adopting energy-efficient measures, such as LED lighting and smart climate control systems, which lower the club’s monthly utility bills. In addition, modernizing payment processing capabilities to accept contactless and mobile options meets rising consumer expectations and enhances overall convenience. Collectively, these automation initiatives establish a leaner, more flexible operational model, ultimately improving member satisfaction and reinforcing Courts Plus’s competitive edge in a crowded market.

Elevating Revenue Through Strategic Growth

Beyond cutting costs, Courts Plus can boost revenues by introducing a suite of new offerings and engagement tactics. First and foremost, the introduction of 24/7 access appeals to a wider range of lifestyles—from shift workers to early-morning exercisers—thus opening the door to new membership segments. By introducing premium membership tiers, the club can upsell added benefits and exclusive access to specialized classes or reserved equipment, tapping into a clientele willing to pay more for an enhanced experience.

Strategic digital marketing, including targeted social media campaigns and influencer partnerships, will attract younger demographics and raise the club’s visibility in the local community. Meanwhile, expanding military-focused programs can unlock a significant untapped market, given the club’s proximity to nearby bases. Building on this approach, Courts Plus can also pursue corporate partnerships with local businesses interested in wellness initiatives for their employees. In doing so, the club diversifies its revenue streams and positions itself as the leading fitness destination for diverse segments—from corporate teams to military personnel.

Through a calculated blend of automation, improved scheduling, and proactive facility maintenance, labor expenses are forecasted to drop by 20%, utilities by 15%, and long-term maintenance costs will decline due to more efficient upkeep.

Projected Cost Reductions

VI. Marketing & Social Media Presence

In today’s fitness industry, a strong digital presence is vital for driving membership growth and retention. Yet, Courts Plus currently lags behind its local competitors in social media engagement and online visibility. This limited internet footprint not only reduces brand awareness but also hinders the club’s ability to attract new members.

Current Social Media Metrics

As of now, Courts Plus has just 262 followers on Instagram, significantly trailing behind nearby gyms such as Gold’s Gym Jacksonville (2,381), Onslow Fitness (2,355), Fit 4 Life Jacksonville (3,151), and the specialized Iron Forged CrossFit (13,100). Facebook engagement also remains minimal, with no targeted advertising campaigns currently in place. Meanwhile, the club holds a 3.8-star rating on Google Reviews but seldom responds to member feedback, missing an opportunity to improve online reputation. Compounding these issues, there is $0 allocated for social media marketing, leaving significant gaps in brand visibility and engagement.
