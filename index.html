Business Turnaround Plan

Courts Plus Health Club

Addressing Challenges and Driving Sustainable Growth

Executive Summary

Business Overview

Courts Plus is a well-established, 33,900-square-foot health club in Jacksonville, North Carolina, offering comprehensive amenities that include dual pools, five racquetball courts, a basketball court, and extensive workout facilities. The club has demonstrated consistent revenue growth over the past three years: $672,814 in 2022, $731,121 in 2023 (+8.7% year-over-year), and $777,875 in 2024 (+6.4%). With a stable membership base of 1,324 primary units, Courts Plus experienced an overall membership increase of 8% in 2024 alone, underscoring its ongoing appeal in the local community.

Market Opportunity

Located in the Jacksonville MSA—a growing region of approximately 210,531 residents—Courts Plus benefits from a prime location and favorable demographics. The area includes 47,000 active-duty military personnel, yet Courts Plus currently captures only 20.3% of its membership from the military community, indicating a significant opportunity for targeted outreach and specialized offerings. With a median age of 26.4 and a median household income of $72,750, the local population aligns strongly with the core health club customer profile. Courts Plus is also the only facility in the area featuring dual pools, giving it a unique competitive edge for both recreational and fitness-minded individuals.

Investment Requirements

A total investment of $2.914M is required to acquire, modernize, and optimize Courts Plus for sustained profitability. This includes a $2.6M purchase price—well below the facility’s $3.15M appraised value—creating immediate equity for investors. Additionally, $150,000 is earmarked for facility updates, $64,400 for automation systems, and $100,000 set aside as working capital. Together, these targeted investments will ensure a swift transition into a leaner, more modern fitness operation primed for continued revenue growth.

Growth Strategy

The modernization plan prioritizes automated access and membership management systems, enabling 24/7 operations that cater to broader member schedules. Facility upgrades will address $99,539 in identified repairs, enhancing both safety and appeal. By developing enhanced military and corporate programs, Courts Plus can tap into substantial untapped markets, while simultaneously improving operational efficiency to reduce monthly utilities (currently $7,500) and optimize annual insurance costs ($63,032 on record). These strategic steps will position Courts Plus for long-term stability, higher profit margins, and a stronger foothold in the thriving Jacksonville MSA.

II. Current Business Analysis

Operational Roadblocks

Courts Plus Health Club continues to face operational inefficiencies that inflate expenses and constrain growth. Manual processes for member check-ins, billing, and maintenance scheduling create significant labor overhead, with staff costs ranging from $10 per hour for front desk staff to $20 per hour for management. This staffing structure includes a full-time Club Manager/GM, Assistant Manager at $15 per hour, two MOD team members at $12.50 per hour, and part-time maintenance at $16 per hour. While maintaining revenue growth to $777,875 in 2024, operating losses of $38,567 persist—though significantly improved from $320,590 in 2022.

Beyond labor costs, mounting overhead expenses including $7,500 in monthly utilities, $63,032 in annual insurance, and $62,913 in taxes and licenses strain profit margins. Technology costs of $680 monthly plus 5% processing fees for Club Auto, alongside urgent infrastructure repairs totaling $99,539, demand immediate attention. Operating hour limitations further constrain growth potential in a market where 24/7 access has become standard.

Market Landscape & Competitive Analysis

Courts Plus operates in the Jacksonville Metropolitan Statistical Area, serving a population of 210,531 residents with a median age of 26.4 years and median household income of $72,750. The market includes 47,000 active duty military personnel, presenting significant growth potential beyond the current 20.3% military membership penetration. The area's young demographic profile and steady military presence provide a stable base for expansion.

The competitive landscape includes established chains Planet Fitness, Anytime Fitness, and Crunch Fitness, which offer 24/7 access and modern digital amenities. However, Courts Plus maintains distinct advantages with its 33,900-square-foot facility featuring unique amenities: an indoor Junior Olympic pool (75' x 35'), outdoor pool (60' x 59'), five racquetball courts (40' x 20' each), and full-size basketball court (66.5' x 58'). This comprehensive offering positions Courts Plus as the only full-service, dual-pool facility in the market.

Membership Analysis & Revenue Structure

Current membership of 1,324 primary units demonstrates strong market acceptance, with 1,211 members choosing long-term contracts—indicating high satisfaction and value perception. Revenue distribution shows a healthy mix: 41% from two-year contracts, 26% from corporate memberships, 18% from month-to-month agreements, and 15% from one-year contracts. Despite averaging 55 monthly cancellations, overall membership grew 8% in 2024, reflecting strong market demand and effective retention strategies.

Growth Opportunities & Market Positioning

Courts Plus's market position offers multiple growth vectors. The military market remains significantly undertapped, with potential to more than double current penetration. Well-maintained equipment from 2016/2017 provides a solid foundation for premium positioning, while the club's comprehensive amenities justify higher price points. The facility's current membership distribution, class attendance patterns (peaking at 32 participants), and stable corporate membership base (26% of revenue) demonstrate strong potential for expanded programming and enhanced corporate partnerships.

Revenue trends show consistent growth: $672,814 in 2022, $731,121 in 2023 (+8.7%), and $777,875 in 2024 (+6.4%). This growth, coupled with improving operational efficiency—evidenced by reduced losses from $320,590 to $38,567—positions Courts Plus to capitalize on market opportunities through strategic modernization and expanded service offerings.

III. Modernization Opportunities

Boosting Efficiency Through Automation

Courts Plus Health Club has a powerful opportunity to drive down overhead and streamline operations by adopting modern technology. Automated access control systems, designed to enable 24/7 facility entry, substantially reduce staffing needs and cater to members who prefer off-peak workout hours. In parallel, digital membership management software eliminates manual tasks—like billing and check-ins—freeing staff to focus on member support and retention rather than administrative duties. Enhanced tools such as automated scheduling and self-service kiosks further minimize labor-intensive processes, helping Courts Plus manage escalating costs, including a monthly utility bill of $7,500 and annual insurance fees of $63,032.

Additional savings can be realized by upgrading to energy-efficient fixtures—like LED lighting and smart climate control—to combat rising utility expenses. Modernizing payment processing to include contactless and mobile options meets growing consumer expectations for convenience and security. Taken together, these automation efforts create a lean, future-ready operational framework that amplifies member satisfaction, reduces strain on front-line staff, and repositions Courts Plus as a competitive leader in the Jacksonville market.

Elevating Revenue Through Strategic Growth

While automation cuts costs, Courts Plus can also accelerate revenue by rolling out innovative new services. Initiating 24/7 access appeals to working professionals and early risers, broadening the membership pool. Introducing premium membership tiers allows the club to upsell additional benefits—such as exclusive classes, advanced booking privileges, or upgraded amenities—to members willing to pay for a best-in-class experience.

To reach younger demographics and the extensive military market (currently only 20.3% of total membership), Courts Plus must invest in targeted digital campaigns and influencer collaborations. Strengthening partnerships with nearby military bases and local businesses can unlock lucrative corporate wellness opportunities, helping diversify the club’s revenue streams and drive consistent growth. By capitalizing on these avenues, Courts Plus can leverage its existing membership base of 1,324 (which grew by 8% in 2024), seize untapped markets, and lay the foundation for enduring success as Jacksonville’s premier fitness destination.

V. Financial Projections

Courts Plus Health Club’s financial projections illustrate a clear path toward profitability, hinging on targeted cost reductions and a focus on revenue-generating initiatives. By investing in automation, scheduling optimizations, and proactive facility maintenance, labor expenses are expected to drop by 20% while utilities could decline by 15%, partly offsetting the club’s current $7,500 monthly bill. Enhanced maintenance protocols will further drive down long-term repair costs, contributing to a leaner operational framework.

Projected Cost Reductions

These savings stem from automating manual tasks, upgrading climate controls for energy efficiency, and deploying advanced infrastructure solutions that reduce the need for ongoing, ad hoc repairs. When viewed collectively, such measures position Courts Plus to more effectively manage overhead, laying the groundwork for improved financial stability and a faster return on modernization investments.

Projected Revenue Growth

Concurrently, membership is poised to grow by 25% as 24/7 access and enhanced marketing reach new customer segments—particularly younger demographics and local military personnel. Additional revenue streams will stem from personal training services, corporate wellness partnerships, and premium membership tiers offering exclusive benefits. Supporting this expansion, streamlined digital management will bolster cash flow, ensuring that revenue gains translate into sustained profitability over the coming year.

VI. Marketing & Social Media Presence

In today’s fitness industry, a robust digital presence is critical for driving membership growth and retention. Yet, Courts Plus trails its local competitors in both social media engagement and online visibility—limiting brand awareness and impeding the club’s ability to attract new members.

Current Social Media Metrics

Currently, Courts Plus maintains only 262 Instagram followers, falling behind nearby gyms such as Gold’s Gym Jacksonville (2,381), Onslow Fitness (2,355), Fit 4 Life Jacksonville (3,151), and the specialized Iron Forged CrossFit (13,100). Facebook engagement is also minimal, with no targeted advertising campaigns. Compounding these challenges, the club’s 3.8-star Google Reviews rating remains largely unoptimized, as management seldom responds to member feedback—missing a prime opportunity to refine its online reputation. Moreover, a social media marketing budget of $0 severely restricts Courts Plus from reaching its full promotional potential.

Action Plan for Digital Growth

Courts Plus will initiate a comprehensive social media overhaul designed to showcase the club’s unique amenities and invigorate member interaction. Daily content updates, member success stories, and behind-the-scenes facility highlights will keep audiences engaged while underscoring the club’s value. Building relationships with local fitness influencers and responding promptly to online reviews will further solidify Courts Plus’s digital reputation, fostering a community-centered brand. Over the long term, this strategic expansion of the club’s online footprint will drive member acquisition, elevate brand loyalty, and position Courts Plus as Jacksonville’s premier choice for fitness and wellness.

VII. Conclusion & Investment Recap

Courts Plus Health Club now stands at a pivotal juncture, with this Turnaround Plan offering a practical blueprint to move from financial instability toward lasting profitability. By combining modernization initiatives, cost-saving automation, and carefully targeted market expansion, the club is poised to regain competitiveness and deliver sustainable growth.
